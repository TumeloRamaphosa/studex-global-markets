Trade Corridors

A corridor is the unit we actually manage: an origin, a load port, a discharge port, an incoterm, a lead time and a settlement rail. The rail is assigned at the corridor level, not the lot level — because it is the counterparty jurisdiction, not the commodity, that decides how money is allowed to move.

Settlement Architecture

Two rails, chosen by corridor

Agricultural goods are broadly exempt from the sanctions programmes that touch our Russian corridors — the constraint has never been the cargo, it is the banking. That is a real reason to want faster rails, and a bad reason to route every corridor through one. Studex assigns the rail per corridor and gates the documentary ones behind screening.

Rail A — On-chainT+0 / T+1

Assigned where no corridor participant sits in a restricted jurisdiction. East African origin to African, Gulf or EU destination. This is where stablecoin settlement earns its keep: it removes correspondent-bank latency on exactly the routes where correspondent banking is slowest and most expensive.

Open items. FSCA crypto-asset service provider licensing on the South African leg, and SARB exchange-control reporting on any cross-border flow. Both are workable; neither is done. Treat Rail A as design-complete and licence-pending.
Rail B — Documentary30–90 days

Mandatory for every corridor with a restricted-jurisdiction participant — which today means all Russian-origin grain, wheat, meat and poultry, and all coffee sold into Russian buyers. Slower by design. The delay is where the screening happens, and the paper is what makes the trade defensible.

1Counterparty screeningOFAC SDN · EU consolidated · UK HMT. Blocking gate — no instrument issues before it clears.
2Instrument sized to ticketLC above $50–200K · Gulf intermediary $100–500K · TT below $50–100K
3Documentary set assembledBill of lading · certificate of origin · phytosanitary · GOST-R or Halaal where applicable
4Third-party inspectionSGS or Bureau Veritas. Lloyds marine cover in place.
5Presentation and paymentAgainst compliant documents at the issuing bank
Non-negotiable. Rail B corridors never route through Rail A, and no corridor may be re-assigned from B to A to shorten settlement. Bridging a restricted-jurisdiction payment onto stablecoin rails is the specific pattern regulators treat as evasion, regardless of whether the cargo is exempt.

Agent Fleet

Who runs the desk

The exchange is operated by agents, one per lifecycle state, each with a defined input, a defined output and a defined limit. Two of them can stop a trade. None of them can move money on their own — settlement release requires an inspection certificate and, on Rail B, a cleared screening record that an agent may write but never overwrite.

Runtime Assessment
OpenFangAdopt as runtime

Rust agent OS — kernel, runtime, memory, skill host, ~40 channel adapters, WASM sandboxing and Merkle audit trails. Domain-neutral, which is exactly why it fits: no equities bias to fight. The channel adapters matter more than they look — WhatsApp, Telegram and email are how origination and broker traffic actually move in East Africa and the Gulf.

Cost: pre-1.0, breaking changes between minor versions — pin commits. Every custom skill is Rust or a WASM boundary; that is a staffing commitment if the team is Python or TypeScript.

ai-hedge-fundPattern reference only

Its value is the topology, not the code: many opinionated analysts score the same object in parallel, a risk gate filters, one allocator decides. That shape transfers cleanly to scoring a lot or a counterparty from several angles. The agents themselves do not — they are stock-picking heuristics keyed to a ticker.

Mismatch: physical coffee has no ticker and no continuous mark. Every agent's input contract would be rewritten. Also self-described as educational, not for real trading.

cashclaw (npm package)Excluded from settlement

Not to be confused with our own Cashclaw agent below. The package is client tooling for a freelance marketplace — thirteen skills, all producing digital marketing deliverables. Its one transferable idea is Guard: YAML cost caps, recursion detection and tool firewalls, a sensible control model for any agent touching money.

Hard block: its rail is Stripe plus autonomous agent-to-agent USDC. Pointing that at a Russia-leg corridor is the precise exposure Rail B exists to prevent. Reference Guard; wire nothing.

The gap none of the three fills: origination, trade documents — bill of lading, phytosanitary, certificate of origin, GOST-R, Halaal — incoterms, logistics and trade credit. That layer is ours to build regardless of which runtime we pick.

Counterparties & Screening

Deal-ability is our composite of capability, documentation readiness, network position and infrastructure density. Screening status is separate and absolute — a counterparty can score well on deal-ability and still be gated. The two never trade off against each other.

CounterpartyRoleSupplies / BuysDeskRailScreeningDeal-AbilityStage
Screening is a gate, not a score. REQUIRED means the counterparty sits in or transacts through a restricted jurisdiction and must clear OFAC SDN, EU consolidated and UK HMT lists — plus a beneficial-ownership check — before any instrument is issued. It is not an allegation about that counterparty; it is the standard our own banking correspondents will apply to us. CLEAR means screened with no adverse finding on the date shown in the file. Screening is re-run per shipment, never once per relationship.

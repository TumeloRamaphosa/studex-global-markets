### Revenue Sharing Model

**Option A: Transaction Fee**
- Studex takes 2% of each trade
- Bitfury receives 0.5% data sourcing fee
- Producer keeps 97.5%

**Option B: Subscription Revenue**
- Tier pricing ($99–custom)
- 70% to Studex operations
- 15% to Bitfury (data + integration)
- 15% to regional governments/NGOs

**Option C: Hybrid**
- Small trades: transaction fee
- Bulk/recurring: subscription
- Both models active
